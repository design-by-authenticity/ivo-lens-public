## What fundamentally changed since v3.2

1. **Function-per-scope architecture**  
   O, V, and I are assigned to functions or aspects within an explicit analytical scope. They are not ontological classifications of whole entities.

2. **Tightened I-definition**  
   Situated Selectivity requires structural openness within the reconstructed scope.

3. **Positive evidence**  
   Unexplained behavior is not automatically I. An I-function requires positive evidence of a selective step.

4. **Revocability**  
   I-assignments are evidence- and scope-bound and may be revised when a stronger scope-matched reconstruction supports that change.

5. **Cross-scale non-cancellation**  
   An O/V reconstruction at another scale does not automatically revoke a valid I-reconstruction. Scale-indexed reconstructions may coexist without metaphysical claims of irreducibility.

6. **Domain terminology safeguard**  
   Terms such as *selection*, *choice*, *recognition*, *filtering*, and *function* do not by themselves establish an I-function.

Version 4.0 results from systematic falsification and cross-domain stress testing. The release candidate series is preserved under [`../prompts/development-history/v4.0/`](../prompts/development-history/v4.0/) as non-canonical provenance; it is not a substitute for a future full research dossier.
