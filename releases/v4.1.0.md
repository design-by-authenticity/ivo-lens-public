## Status

I·V·O Lens Prompt v4.1 is the canonical successor to the frozen v4.0 release. The meanings of O — Structured Possibility, V — Instantiated Relation, and I — Situated Selectivity are unchanged. Version 4.1 is an epistemic-safeguard release rather than an operator-semantic revision.

## Why v4.1 exists

Cross-model Project Hessdalen runs exposed reproducible errors around the reconstruction rather than failures of the O/V/I operator semantics themselves. The main failures included observation-to-object/class promotion, hypothesized mechanisms treated as established O, provenance inflation, false negative I-claims, incompatible status vocabularies, indiscriminate uncertainty propagation, and synthesis/reliability language exceeding claim-level evidence.

## Main additions

1. Shared evidence provenance: `SUPPLIED`, `VERIFIED`, `REPORTED`, `BACKGROUND`, `INFERRED`.
2. Shared claim status: `ESTABLISHED`, `SUPPORTED`, `HYPOTHESIS`, `UNRESOLVED`, `ABSENT`, `DEPENDENT(→A)`.
3. Observation → event → class → membership → mechanism separation.
4. Unknown-state and negative-claim safeguard.
5. Hypothesized-mechanism gate: hypothesized O is not established O.
6. Evidential dependency gate.
7. Causal-status safeguard within V.
8. Claim-level reliability as primary over global ratings.
9. Synthesis may not increase claim strength.
10. Unresolved analysis defaults to information gain rather than intervention.
11. Canonical identity/disambiguation guard.

## Regression status

The frozen RC2 candidate was run unchanged across Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini under a controlled condition with no external source base. The major targeted Hessdalen failure modes were prevented across models. Residual variance remained around the distinction between epistemic ambiguity and structural openness in human classification; the existing v4.1 rules already address this boundary, so no further semantic change was introduced before release.

The exact RC2 artifact is preserved under `prompts/development-history/v4.1/` for reproducibility.

## Scope

Successful regression is evidence only for the tested failure modes and conditions. It does not establish universal validity, completeness, scientific validation, or immunity to model bias.
