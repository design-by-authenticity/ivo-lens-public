## Experimental distinction

The protocol separates **I-position**, **I-function**, and **I-autonomy**, with **mechanistic implementation** recorded as a separate reconstruction dimension. Under its experimental semantics, complete mechanistic explanation does not automatically revoke an evidenced selective function; residual structural openness is assessed under autonomy. O/V explanatory structure and evidence safeguards remain part of the proposed test design.

The source requires independently supported selection evidence, minimal carriers, remove-and-compare checks, and revocation conditions. It includes 12 failure checks and explicitly distinguishes missing mechanistic knowledge from evidence of autonomy.

The source records **PASS WITH OPEN POSITION BOUNDARY**, retention of the experimental split, and a decision to proceed to an experimental v0.4 draft. Its primary open issue is the positive boundary for I-position; partial causation, redundant selection, nested selection, and scale/scope interaction also remain open. This publication records the supplied decision; it does not create v0.4 or revise canonical v4.1.

Material qualifications in the supplied results include:

- NM-07 (historical register) is a provisional position result; memory alone is not established as sufficient.
- NM-07B uses `I-position NOT ESTABLISHED`, a label outside the archived v0.3 protocol's listed position-status vocabulary. The supplied wording is preserved, not silently normalised.
- NM-04 reports autonomy SUPPORTED only within the stated stochastic model; it does not establish an unrestricted autonomy claim.
- NM-08 distinguishes an established internal selection function from a function NOT ESTABLISHED for actual project execution at the broader scope.
- AB-03 is reported as PASS with correction, with PARTIAL implementation and UNRESOLVED autonomy.
- AB-04 now reports COMPLETE implementation and autonomy NOT ESTABLISHED, whereas the earlier A/B summary reported partial implementation and unresolved autonomy. Both historical records remain intact; exact run inputs and outputs would be needed to assess that change.

The report describes reruns after stricter boundaries were established, but full original inputs/outputs, execution metadata, model versions, and a run-by-run record of corrections or instruction changes were not supplied. The archive therefore does not independently verify unchanged-run conditions, the PASS assessments, comparative superiority, or cross-model reproducibility. No tests were rerun for this archival update.

The proposed next phase adds partial-causation, redundant-selector, nested-selection, and further position-boundary cases alongside the existing suites and cross-model checks. These are recorded proposals, not completed tests.
