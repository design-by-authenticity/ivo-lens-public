## Draft focus

The supplied protocol separates I-position, I-function, mechanistic implementation, and I-autonomy. It makes the relevant continuation and analytical scope explicit, adds a mandatory causal-contribution gate, strengthens the position challenge beyond ordinary O/V connectivity, and addresses partial causation, redundant selectors, nested selection, and scale interaction.

Its position-status vocabulary explicitly includes both NOT ESTABLISHED and NOT REQUIRED. Mechanistic completeness neither establishes nor revokes a function under the experimental proposal; autonomy is assessed separately. These are descriptions of the supplied experimental draft, not adopted canonical definitions.
