Record the following as the reviewed experimental finding:

> Downstream causal consequence, admission/exclusion, threshold sensitivity, or classification alone are insufficient for I-function.

> Relational allocation among multiple concurrently relevant alternatives, where selection of one changes the selectable status of another and causally affects continuation, provides sufficient positive evidence for I-function in the tested structure.

**Sufficient does not mean necessary.** The source's statement that an I-function requires relational allocation is not accepted as a universal definition. Neither competition, scarce slots, nor cross-candidate dependence has been shown necessary for every possible I-function. A noncompetitive ACCEPT/REJECT process with independent commitment evidence remains a case to investigate, not automatically a positive or negative function.

Absence of competition alone therefore cannot justify rejecting all other candidate functions. The negative A/B decisions concern the supplied independent gating structures and their evidence. Mechanistic determinism alone is not a negative criterion.
